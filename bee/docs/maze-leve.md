# Maze Level - Windy Woods

After the user completes the level with the bear, the next unlocked level is Windy Woods, which
is a maze.

Generate a fairly large maze randomly each time, so it cannot be learned.  The walls of the maze are made up of trees, which blow in the breeze,
with red/brown leaves sometimes fluttering down.  The angle of the camera is a bit lower than usual so it's behind the bee following it.

The bee drops yellow pollen behind it to mark where it has already been.  At the end of each dead end is a flower which the bee eats when 
coming near to it.  Animate the eating, then zoom the camera high above the maze to show the full maze for 5 seconds, then zoom back in to the bee.

When the bee eats the flower, a glowing line line a scent in the air appears, leading from the flower along the correct path to completing the maze. 
This can be seen from the high up zoomed out view, and when back zoomed in.  This stays forever, with glowing pulses slowly moving in the correct
direction of travel.  The purpose is to guide the bee in the right direction for a bit, but not too long.  Each time they are lost, there should
be a flower at each dead end which repeats the process.   Eating a flower also shows a burst of colours like other times.

When they get to the end of the maze, show another burst of colours and the level is complete.
