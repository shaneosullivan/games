## What is it?

It's a game where you are a caterpillar and you eat everything. The more you eat the bigger and fatter you get. You start off eating leaves, berries and grass.

A new game.

## How do you play?

_What does the player do?
You're aim is to get bigger and eat as much as you want.What are the controls, The controls are the same as the bee game. the goal, eat 80 leaves, 40 flowers, 60 berries and 45 fruits like apples, wild strawberries, blackberries and peaches.. how do you win or lose? You don't lose this game. You win by eating so much that you turn into a butterfly.
The game Finishes when you turn into a butterfly.
