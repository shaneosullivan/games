## What is it?

It's a game where you are a flying squirrel and you fly through obstacles. It's based on wing suit flying.
_One or two sentences: what is Squirrel Glider, and who is it for?_
Squirrel glider is based on wing suit gliding and it is for kids mostly, though adults may enjoy it too.
A new game.

## How do you play?

You fly through obstacles as if you were wearing a wing suit but you are a flying squirrel.
_What does the player do? What are the controls, the goal, and how do you win or
finish?_
The player flies around as a flying squirrel through obstacles

## What does it look like?

You are looking at the back of a squirrel, the squirrel is in the centre of the screen, you also see its surroundings
_The setting, the mood, the main things on screen._
You are in the mountains,The mood is calm, the main things on screen are the flying squirrel and its surroundings

## What's in the first version?

You are a flying squirrel and you start at the edge of a huge cliff. You tap the screen or hit the space bar to jump off the edge, then fly in a physics accurate way as if in a wing suit. It should turn/bank realistically.
Use the same up/down/left/right controls as in the bee and caterpillar game. Down raises the head and slows down. Up points the squirrel's head towards the ground and speeds up the descent. Left and right banks left and right.
_The smallest thing worth playing. List what has to be there, and what can wait._
There has to be a flying squirrel.
