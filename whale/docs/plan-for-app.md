## What is it?

It is a game where you swim around as a whale.
_One or two sentences: what is Whale, and who is it for?_
Whale is a game where you swim around and eat fish. Its for kids but adults can enjoy it too.

## How do you play?

You have the same controls as in the bee game and you swim around as a whale eating fish.
_What does the player do? What are the controls, the goal, and how do you win or finish?_
The player swims around the ocean and eats fish and if you eat plastic you restart. You Finnish when you make it to the end.

## What does it look like?

It looks cute and friendly and the whale should look like a beluga whale.
_The setting, the mood, the main things on screen._
The setting is in the sea with lots of coral with apples sunlight like theres real water above. some of the sea is deeper than some other bits. The mood is friendly and not scary. The main thing on screen is the beluga whale.

## What's in the first version?

You are a cute beluga whale and you eat all the fish. You should only eat the fish that touch your head. Some fish run away. Some bits of the ocean are deep and others are shallow.
_The smallest thing worth playing. List what has to be there, and what can wait._
Just swimming around with coral and sand on the ground with dappled sunlight on the coral, beluga whale and sand like there's water above with small waves. The whale should be able to swim up to the top of the water and also the bottom of the sea. The controls should be like the bee game ones.
